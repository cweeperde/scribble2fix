Welcome to Scribble2Fix

Scribble on your screen to show an AI coding tool what to change.

  1. Press your hotkey. The screen freezes and you can draw on it.
  2. Mark what you want: circle, scribble, draw boxes or arrows.
  3. Press Enter. The annotated screenshot is copied, ready to paste into Claude, Codex or any chat.

Every color has a meaning

Add something new here 1 Remove this 2 Change or move this (arrows: from here to there) 3

Shortcuts

Text, numbers, eraser and size are folded away behind the › button in the toolbar.

Your hotkey

Press it anywhere to start a scribble. Click the box to pick a different one.

Start Scribble2Fix at loginIt waits in the system tray, so your hotkey always works.

You can change all of this later: tray icon → Settings.

Connect your AI tools

Recommended for full functionality. Then:

  • type /scribble to send your latest capture, without pasting
  • the AI can ask you for a scribble when it needs to see what you mean (it always asks first)
